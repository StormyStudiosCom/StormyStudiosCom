A few good questions

Open an answer. Keep as many open as you need.

What belongs in a small business FAQ?

Start with questions customers actually ask: what is included, how long it takes, what happens next and how to get help. Give a direct answer first, then add any useful detail.

Keep essential information, such as prices or opening hours, visible where people need it elsewhere on the site too.

Does this need JavaScript?

No. The HTML details and summary elements provide the disclosure behaviour. CSS handles the layout, focus treatment and small icon change.

Use Tab to reach a question, then Enter or Space to open or close it.

Why can I open several answers?

Someone comparing details can keep the relevant answers together. Opening another question does not close the answer they were reading.

The page grows naturally around the content. There is no fixed answer height or separate scrolling area.

How can I make this fit my website?

Replace these examples with your own questions and answers, then adjust the colour variables and type sizes. Keep the whole question clickable and retain a visible keyboard focus style.

Try your longest answer on a narrow screen and with enlarged text. The small icon transition is disabled when a visitor prefers reduced motion.
